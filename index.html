This form is not monitored and is not for emergencies. Seek immediate care (call 911 or go to an emergency room) for new or severe chest pain, difficulty breathing, fainting, stroke symptoms (face drooping, arm weakness, speech trouble, sudden vision or balance changes), black or bloody stools, a severe allergic reaction, or any rapidly worsening symptom.
⚠️ Complete Other Forms First
This form is designed for complex cases and works alongside your MC Scorecard and Lifestyle & Medical History intake forms. Please complete those first. This form covers areas those forms do not — pathogens, co-infections, mold/CIRS, MCAS, POTS, spike protein / long COVID, and heavy metal toxicity.
📋 How to Fill Out This Form
  • Rate each symptom based on how often you experienced it over the last 90 days
  • For Yes/No questions, select what applies
  • Scores update automatically — Yellow or Red flags an area for your consultation
  • ★ Hallmark items count double
  • When finished, go to Step 8 — Review & Print to see all your scores
  • Click Email Results to send your scores and full answers file directly to your practitioner, or use Save to File to keep a copy on your device
  • You can also Copy Summary to clipboard or Print / Save as PDF
⚠ Important Disclaimer
Higher scores indicate a stronger pattern fit for discussion and possible follow-up — not a diagnosis. Many symptoms overlap across conditions. This form helps guide follow-up questions and testing priorities.
🔒 Your Privacy
Your answers never leave your device. This form runs entirely in your browser — no data is sent to any server, no information is stored in the cloud, and no one can access your responses unless you choose to share them. All data is saved locally on your computer. You decide what to share, when, and with whom — whether that’s saving a file, printing a PDF, or emailing your results. You are in full control of your data.
